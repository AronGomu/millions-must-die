Architecture · Phase 2 · ADR 022 / ADR 023

Combat prototype

Weapons, damage, turrets, enemy AI as one vertical slice on the RTS world. Enemies are RTS entities under OWNER_ENEMY = 1 — same store, same hard bodies, same pooled flow fields, same state hash. The horde sim/ is untouched and this page claims nothing about it: hundreds of enemies here are not phase 3's tens of thousands.

Where combat sits in the tick

Damage, cooldowns, death

  • Instant-hit on cooldown. No projectile entities. Target in weapon range + cooldown 0 → apply_damage the same tick, cooldown resets. Per hit: max(1, damage − armor).
  • Columns and tables. HP is a u32 store column; armor and max HP are kind tables (pure functions of kind). Cooldown is a u32 column. HP, cooldowns and the kill/loss counters enter state_hash fixed-width.
  • Death. DamageResult = Damaged | Killed | Indestructible | NoTarget; nodes indestructible. Units despawn (generational ids make stale handles resolve None). Buildings — HQ included — un-stamp their footprint (invalidating every cached field, the same rule as stamping), cancel their production queue with no refund, revoke their supply grant. HQ death clears start_hq and idles gatherers needing drop-off. Selection prune stays last in the tick.

Targeting and firing rules

OrderFires?Behavior
IdleyesAuto-acquire nearest enemy in range, fire in place. Never chases.
AttackMove (tag 5)yesDescend shared field toward goal; halt and fire while a target is in range; resume on kill.
Attack (tag 4)yesClose distance to the target, fire when in range; dead target → Idle (player) / resume march (enemy).
Move / Gather / BuildneverA plain Move is a pure reposition — the retreat tool.
Turret (no order)yesStatic firer via building_weapon; range measured from the footprint rect; unfinished sites never fire.

Target selection is deterministic everywhere: nearest valid enemy-of-owner in range, lowest-slot tie-break, ascending-slot iteration. Range to a unit is center distance minus body radius; to a building, distance to the footprint rectangle.

Enemy AI — one rule, one field

March on the objective; attack the first player thing in weapon range. The objective is the approach cell of the nearest player building — a pooled field cannot target a blocked footprint cell — starting at the HQ, retargeting on death events only (cached, no per-tick scan), lowest-slot tie-break, Idle when no player building remains. Every idle enemy is assigned AttackMove at the objective, so hundreds of Ghouls share one pooled flow field: "no per-enemy pathfinding" survives contact with an army, and NAV_FIELD_SLOTS = 8 stands.

Enemies use the ordinary hard-body contract — proposal/commit movement, bounded push chains, and they are ordinary hard pairs: ADR 021's gather-worker exception is not widened. The horde's soft separation (ADR 009) is a different contract on a different store, and no document may merge the two claims.

Player surface

  • Commands. cmd_attack_target / cmd_attack_move / cmd_stop. A arms attack-targeting (click enemy = target, click ground = attack-move); right-click an enemy attacks directly; Stop idles. Attack = card slot 3, Stop = slot 4 — positional keys, zero new script tokens.
  • Feedback. HP bars are texture-free overlay line instances (the grid/drag-box primitive) over damaged ∪ selected entities, hidden at full HP; death is a 12-frame procedural ring; the minimap gains red enemy dots. None of it enters the world hash; offscreen exit lines stay byte-identical.
  • Audio. Attack accepts voice as the existing order cue; rejects as the reject cue. No new bus. Turret fire is silent this phase — a named gap.

The gate, and why waves-only

Pre-placed enemies were proven infeasible (ADR 023): any reachable pre-placed Ghoul reaches the base by ~tick 810; ten kill the 400-HP HQ by ~tick 1170 — inside the phase-1 scripts' pinned windows and the CLI contract's 3000-frame pins, which cannot be honestly re-baselined.

The tracked scene therefore takes waves only: two far-edge spawn points, 400 Ghouls (12 at tick 3000, then 150/150/88 at 4100/4160/4220). First contact lands ≈ tick 3480 — after every pinned window — so both phase-1 scripts' asserted counts are provably unchanged and the one-time re-baseline collapses to a sidecar regeneration plus new zero-valued token pins. The combat script (~4500 frames) demonstrates a soldier-ordered kill, an auto-acquire kill, a turret kill and ≥ 1 player loss, and the exit line appends kills losses enemies_spawned first_combat_tick hq_alive. first_combat_tick > 3000 is the pinned proof that combat begins by marching into range, not by adjacency at spawn.

Long-horizon engine tests that outlive tick 3000 repoint to a byte-identical enemy-free baseline fixture, so they keep testing what they always tested. Phase-0 contracts — the 5000-agent exit hash, collision scenes, render goldens, atlas_count: 4 — do not move.

What this phase does not claim

  • No horde-scale enemies (phase 3), no walls or wave design (phase 4), no balance (phase 5), no missions or win/lose (phase 6).
  • No performance number of any kind — performance stays unmeasured by policy.
  • One enemy kind, melee only; no ranged enemy, no worker attack, no damage types, no projectiles.
  • Nothing here claims a window appeared or a sound was heard — human checklist items stay human.

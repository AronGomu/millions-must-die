Placement, end to end

Order feedback is derived state

Nothing stores "what you last clicked". Every frame, the packer and the card read the live Order and describe it. That is why a target ring reappears when you re-select a worker, and why an order changed by a script, a rally or the combat system is described correctly without any extra bookkeeping.

Ring, not a second colour

Target rings use the selection ring's green at half its band width. One hue means "the world drew this shape for you"; thickness carries the difference between selected and targeted.

Markers are hashed

Ground-order flags are bounded (MAX_MOVE_MARKERS), self-expiring (MOVE_MARKER_TICKS) and fed into state_hash — a feedback affordance two processes could disagree about would be a determinism hole.

Bearing, not route

The dashed line is a straight segment to the unit's own formation slot. It answers "where is this going", not "which way around the rock" — a named gap, not an oversight.

Vocabulary gap, recorded

The status line says MINERAL (the user's word) while kind_label says CRYSTAL (the code's). Deliberate, written down, one token to unify.

Follow and rally

  • Follow holds, it does not stack. A follower stops at interaction_reach from the target's shape and re-paths only when the target drifts more than FOLLOW_REPATH_CELLS from the goal it last pathed to — bounded field churn, no per-tick rebuild.
  • A dead target ends the order as Idle on the tick it is noticed, the same discipline a mined-out gather node already gets.
  • Rally onto minerals means gather, because that is what an RTS player means by it — not "walk to a spot near the rocks".
  • Enemies are not rally targets. A rally click on an enemy is rejected with a receipt, so a mis-click is legible instead of silent.

The trapped builder

The reported bug — "the barracks spawned over my worker and I cannot move it" — lands on machinery that already handles exactly that case. finish_site plans an evacuation for every body the finishing footprint covers, and if one evacuee has nowhere legal to go it discards the whole plan and leaves the site walkable at build_ticks - 1.

So the symptom is a failure mode, not a gap — a permanently stalled site, a body sealed by the mask rebuilt after the commit, or an order still pointing at the finished site. The ticket reproduces it first and names which; the user's "phasing" grace period is the fallback design, applied only if the repro proves no legal centre exists.

The sandbox scene

The last item is the one that makes all the others testable by hand: a scene that starts with the base already built. That needs one small schema addition — a declarative list of finished buildings and starting units, both serde-defaulted so every existing scene file stays byte-identical and hash-stable — plus validation that a declared building is square-aligned and overlaps nothing.

rts.buildings[{ kind, cell }], seeded finished and stampedsquare-aligned, in bounds, clear of terrain, other buildings, the HQ and every node

assets/scenarios/rts_sandbox_v1.ron uses both: HQ, Depot, Barracks and two Turrets already standing, six workers, eight soldiers, and thirty authored waves arriving from the two far corners. It runs with --scenario and no --frames, and it is deliberately not on the merge gate — an untimed scene proves nothing on a timer, and its job is to be played.

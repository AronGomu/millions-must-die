As built — three corrections to the diagram above

  1. Push-aside plus a bounded chain. "Accept whole or wait" alone froze the game: a pooled field is body-blind. A mover displaces the bodies it touches along their contact normals, through a chain bounded at MAX_PUSH_DEPTH = 3 and MAX_PUSHED_BODIES = 8, one displacement per body per tick, all-or-nothing. Only after a chain is rejected does it retry its descent rotated −45°, +45°, −90°, +90°. Nothing relaxes an existing overlap.
  2. Adaptive interaction reach. max(interaction_reach(kind), chosen approach-cell distance + NAV_CENTER_TOLERANCE_CELLS); entity_approach_cell returns the cell and its distance.
  3. Known gap, pinned not fixed. The centre-blocked mask and the continuous sweep disagree about a 1–2 cell diagonal channel (y ≈ 172..174, x ≈ 190..199): the field calls it reachable, a 3-cell body wedges. Pre-existing, off the acceptance path, pinned as an #[ignore]d reproducer in crates/mmd-engine/tests/rts_nav_staleness.rs.
